WHY WE CREATED NOVA

Astronomy beyond sight

For centuries people have explored the universe by looking at the stars, but not everyone can experience the sky visually

NOVA makes astronomy more accessible for blind and visually impaired people by transforming astronomical data into sound, narration and interactive experiences
