ABOUT NOVA

Astronomy Beyond Sight.

Most astronomy experiences rely on being able to see the night sky. For people who are blind or visually impaired, this can make exploring the universe much harder.

NOVA changes how people explore the universe. By transforming real astronomical data into sound, speech and interactive experiences, we want to make astronomy accessible for everyone, regardless of sight.
