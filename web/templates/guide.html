YOUR GUIDE TO NOVA

A sky you can hear

NOVA is designed to work with sound, a keyboard, a screen reader, or a mouse

01

Explore the sky

Choose your observing location and leave the date on live or set another date to visit a sky from a different time. Select a constellation to isolate it and start a guided tour right from the sidebar

02

Find a star or planet

Use the searchable star list or explore the map with arrow keys

03

Listen and ask

Choose full audio for music, click sounds and narration, voice only for narration without music, or audio off to mute everything

KEYBOARD ACCESS

Designed for more than sight

Tab Move between the controls and the map
Arrow keys Move between visible stars or planets when the map is focused or when a constellation is selected, arrows navigate only its stars
Enter Hear more about the currently selected object
Escape Clear any selected star or constellation while using the map
Search Look up a star or planet by name instead of using the map

THREE AUDIO MODES

Choose how NOVA sounds

Full audio includes looping ambient music, button-click effects and star narration

Voice only keeps ElevenLabs or browser narration but turns off music and click sounds

Audio off mutes all NOVA audio

Use the audio button at the top of any page to switch modes: Full audio plays music on every page while Voice only keeps narration. Some browsers may wait for your first click before starting music after you switch pages

CHECK YOUR SOUND

Test the audio

This plays your click sound and starts the ambient music so you can check both files without opening the developer console

The test uses your MP3 files in static/sounds

WHAT LIVE MEANS

A calculated view of the sky

NOVA uses catalog positions and astronomy calculations to show where stars and planets should be for your location and time. Live mode refreshes every 30 seconds while the Explore page is open.

Constellation outlines are patterns of connections between stars and their shape moves across your sky because their stars are recalculated, not because the connections themselves change.
