YOUR GUIDE TO NOVA

A sky you can hear

NOVA is designed to work with sound, a keyboard, a screen reader or a mouse

01

Explore the sky

Choose your observing location and leave the date on Live or set another date to visit a different sky

02

Find a star or planet

Use the searchable star list or explore the map with arrow keys and Enter

03

Listen and ask

Turn on audio to hear descriptions or open Ask NOVA to ask an astronomy question aloud

KEYBOARD ACCESS

Designed for more than sight

TabMove between the controls and the map
Arrow keysMove between stars or planets when the map is focused
EnterHear more about the currently selected object
SearchLook up a star or planet by name instead of using the map

WHAT LIVE MEANS

A calculated view of the sky

NOVA uses catalog positions and astronomy calculations to show where stars and planets should be for your location and time Live mode refreshes every 30 seconds while the Explore page is open

Constellation outlines are patterns of connections between stars Their shape moves across your sky because their stars are recalculated, not because the connections themselves change
